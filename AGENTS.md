# Nudge fork (local wellbeing lab)

## Purpose

Personal fork of [astraedus/nudge](https://github.com/astraedus/nudge) for **use-with-consequences** digital wellbeing: stay able to open apps when needed, but make overuse uncomfortable, and earn rare positive unlocks when doing well.

## What matters most (ordered)

1. Per-app **multi-rules**: each rule is **When × Consequence** (not a single binary block).
2. Consequences that hurt without money (delay/grayscale/strip reward/hard block)—always a way to continue if you accept the cost (except optional hard block).
3. Positive reinforcement as a **blank unlock slot** first; fill with something cool later.
4. Apps only (no website/VPN work unless we reopen that).
5. Local rebuild/sideload for this device; keep changes minimal and readable.

## Non-negotiables

- No money / payments / donations as consequences or rewards.
- Do not distribute patched or commercialized builds without explicit say-so.
- Upstream is GPL-3 — derivatives stay free; keep `upstream` remote.
- Explain how to work while working; do not run multi-step install/patch unless asked.
- Do not silently rewrite these soul sections; propose wording and get assent.

## Do not drift into

- AppBlock RE / premium forging (that stays in `android-re-lab`).
- Building a general AppBlock clone (sites, PIN, strict uninstall lock) before When×Consequence works.
- Subscription freemium or Play-store productization of this fork.
